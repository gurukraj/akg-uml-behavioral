AKG behavioral ontology

Namespace: https://w3id.org/akg/behavioral# (prefix akg:) · Version 1.0.0 · License: CC BY 4.0 · Turtle: akg-behavioral.ttl · Repository: gurukraj/akg-uml-behavioral

RDFS vocabulary of the Architectural Knowledge Graph (AKG) for elements and relationships extracted from UML sequence, state machine, activity, communication, and timing diagrams (Table 4 of G. Kulkarni, C. S. Lamba, and S. Solanki, "Architectural Knowledge Graphs from UML Behavioural Models for Early Architectural Bottleneck and Smell Detection").
